# OriginalRoot brand identity

## Position

**From field to home, in view.** OriginalRoot is a local food network where growers, households, and local fulfilment teams can follow the same harvest offer, price breakdown, batch record, and handoff.

The product makes the route easier to see. It does not claim that technology removes every intermediary or verifies every statement. Storage, grading, packing, transport, and coordination are real work; the platform should show their costs clearly.

## Name

**OriginalRoot** points to the source of food and the growers and living soil behind it. It is a working brand name, not a claim of certification, organic status, or exclusive origin.

The name has not received trademark, company-name, domain, or social-handle clearance. A web search surfaced OneRoot, an existing Indian agriculture business with a similar farm-to-business marketplace position. Check for confusion and get qualified clearance before registering the name or promoting it commercially.

## Visual system

- Warm paper, field green, and a restrained harvest-clay accent.
- The mark combines a new shoot with branching roots to signal origin and connection.
- Original vector illustrations link a grower, field, route, and home; avoid generic marketplace stock-photo grids.
- Serif display headlines add a human editorial voice; clear sans-serif body text supports dense operational screens.
- Harvest-story steps, role entry points, crop-group filters, and live offer search turn the product story into usable navigation.
- Motion stays brief and respects reduced-motion settings.

## Product voice

- Say what was recorded, who reviewed it, and what remains unverified.
- Describe availability from current approved offers; do not create artificial scarcity.
- Show grower payout, packing, coordination, route costs, and customer price separately.
- Treat delivery and payment as local operational commitments, not automatic platform guarantees.
